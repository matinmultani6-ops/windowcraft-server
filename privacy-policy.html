Privacy Policy for WindowCraft Pro

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

WindowCraft Pro ("we", "our", or "us") operates the WindowCraft Pro mobile application and calculation suite. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application.

1. Information Collection and Use

WindowCraft Pro does not require users to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (such as your name, phone number, or home address) to use the core calculation and cutting optimization features of the app.

2. Third-Party Ad Services (Google AdMob)

Our application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads). AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP addresses, and app interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our app, please visit: Google Partner Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions Required

The app requests the following device permissions solely for essential functionality:

  • INTERNET & NETWORK STATE: Required to connect to calculation servers and fetch AdMob advertisements.
  • STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Required to save generated PDF calculation reports and quotation bills directly to your device's Downloads directory.

4. Data Security

Your privacy is important to us. All calculation inputs entered into WindowCraft Pro remain stored locally on your device using local storage or processed securely in memory via encrypted HTTPS API requests.

5. Children's Privacy

WindowCraft Pro is intended for professional fabricators, contractors, and adult users (aged 18 and over). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at your registered developer email address.
